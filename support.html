Skis, the ski day planner.

Read this first. Run names, lift times and queue estimates are provisional and are not yet supplied by the resorts. Do not rely on Skis to tell you a lift is still running. Check the resort's own lift status and last-lift times before committing to a route, especially one that crosses a valley.

What Skis is for

Planning a day around the time you have. You say where you are, when you need to be down, and what you are comfortable skiing. It gives you a few routes with different characters, lets you push them around, and then follows you down them.

It is not a shortest-path router. Nobody wants the fastest way down a mountain; they want a good day that ends on time.

Common questions

The map is a simple grey mountain rather than terrain

That is the fallback, drawn from the resort's own altitudes. It appears when real elevation tiles cannot be reached, which usually means no signal. It works entirely offline by design, and the app stays fully usable on it.

It says I am hundreds of kilometres from the resort

Your phone's location is outside the resort, which is what happens when you plan tomorrow from home. Pick a starting point from the list instead.

Location is off, or it asks for https

Location needs permission and a secure connection. If you opened Skis over a plain http address on a local network, the browser refuses location and the app says so rather than blaming the permission. Everything else still works; tap when you arrive at each junction.

Navigation is not advancing on its own

It advances when your phone reports two positions close to the next junction, or one that nothing contradicts for a few seconds. If the signal is poor it will not guess, and the button at the bottom always works.

It says nothing fits

Deliberately. Rather than inventing a route that leaves you on the wrong side of the mountain after the lifts shut, Skis says so and lists what would change it: more time, no lunch, finishing where you started, or opening up a harder grade.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. Saving a route stores the route, the piste graph and the map tiles over it, so a committed day works in airplane mode. That is a requirement rather than a nice-to-have, because alpine coverage is unreliable.

How do I delete my data?

Stats, then Clear history. Or delete the app: nothing is stored anywhere else. See Privacy.

Reporting something

A wrong run name, a lift time that does not match the mountain, or anything that sent you somewhere you did not want to go, is worth reporting. Say which resort, which run or lift, and what it should be.
