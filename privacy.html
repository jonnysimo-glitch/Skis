Privacy

Skis, the ski day planner. Last updated 30 August 2026.

Skis has no account, no login and no server of its own. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere we control, because there is nowhere for it to go.

What stays on your phone

All of it. Specifically:

  • Your location, used to set your starting point to the nearest lift station and to notice when you have reached the next junction while navigating. It is read on the device and used on the device. It is never transmitted and never stored beyond the session.
  • Your ability setting, so you are not asked every time.
  • Your ski history: the days you have finished and their distance, vertical and duration.
  • The route you saved for offline use, so a committed day still works with no signal.

These live in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Clearing your history in Stats deletes the history immediately.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. There is no code in the app that sends your data anywhere, and no third-party SDK that could.

The one thing that does leave your phone

Map tiles. To draw the mountain, the app requests terrain and map imagery from these providers:

  • AWS Terrain Tiles, for elevation
  • MapTiler, for the winter basemap, if the app has been built with a MapTiler key

Those requests say which tiles are wanted, and like any web request they carry your IP address to that provider. That tells them roughly which part of the world is being looked at. It does not include your position, your route, your history or any identifier from the app. Saving a route offline fetches those tiles once so the map keeps working with the radio off.

Children

Skis is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, including them.

Changes

If this ever stops being true, this page changes before the behaviour does, and the app will say so.
